## GDPR

**Role:** Tolka acts as a **data processor** under Article 28. Your organisation is the data controller.

**Lawful basis for processing:** Healthcare providers typically rely on Article 9(2)(h) (healthcare purposes) or Article 6(1)(c) (legal obligation) for processing patient communications. Tolka does not establish your lawful basis — that is your organisation's responsibility.

**Data minimisation:** Tolka's translation pipeline processes text in memory and does not write it to its database, logs or caches. The exception is the optional clinician-flag feature (see [Privacy Architecture](/concepts/privacy-architecture/)).

**Data subject rights:** Tolka holds account metadata, API key records, usage counts and a request audit log for your organisation, and, if you use clinician flags, the text submitted with them. For requests about access or erasure, contact [legal@tolka.health](mailto:legal@tolka.health). The process and timeline are not yet defined.

**International transfers:** The API and Console run in Frankfurt. Free-text translation providers and their transfer terms have not yet been established, and free-text translation is currently off.

**DPA:** A Data Processing Agreement can be requested from [legal@tolka.health](mailto:legal@tolka.health?subject=DPA%20request).

## EU AI Act

Tolka has not completed an EU AI Act assessment and does not claim a risk tier. Design measures in place today:

- **Back-translation check:** for free-text translations, where enabled, the result is translated back and compared with the original.
- **Multi-signal risk scoring:** several signals feed the risk level on a response.
- **Critical overrides:** changes to negations, numbers and medical keywords raise the risk level.
- **Human oversight:** the API lets integrators require clinician acknowledgment for high-risk translations. Tolka is not designed to be used as a fully autonomous clinical translation system.

For the current documentation, contact [compliance@tolka.health](mailto:compliance@tolka.health?subject=AI%20Act%20documentation).
